2-Minute AutoCAD & Civil 3D Setup

Compatible with AutoCAD & AutoCAD Civil 3D 2013 through 2026.

STEP 01

Download & Extract

Download your official ZGE Suite release ZIP and extract the ZGE_Suite.bundle folder.

STEP 02

Copy to ApplicationPlugins

Place ZGE_Suite.bundle into %ProgramData%\Autodesk\ApplicationPlugins\ (or %AppData%\Autodesk\ApplicationPlugins\).

STEP 03

Restart AutoCAD

Launch AutoCAD or Civil 3D — the ZGE Suite VLX binary and toolbars auto-load cleanly.

STEP 04

Activate Once

Type ZGEACT, enter your Z6-... key once, then type ZGE to open the 84-Command Control Center.
