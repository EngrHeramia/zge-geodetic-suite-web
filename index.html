Frequently Asked Questions

          
Direct, transparent answers before you request a license.

        


        

          

            Does it work completely offline?
            
Yes. Activation and all surveying tools run 100% locally inside your Windows AutoCAD or Civil 3D — no internet or cloud login required.

          

          

            Will future suite updates break my license key?
            
No. Your hardware-bound key remains valid across future suite updates on the same PC, and includes a 1-time version rollback option.

          

          

            Are my DWG drawings or TCT files ever uploaded?
            
Never. All computations and exports happen locally on your own workstation in full compliance with RA 10173.

          

          

            What if I reformat my PC or change laptops?
            
Message the developer on Discord or Facebook with your original key and new Machine ID for a verified license transfer.
